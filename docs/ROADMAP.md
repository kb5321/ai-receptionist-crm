# ROADMAP

**Project:** AI Business Assistant Platform  
**Version:** 2.0.0  
**Last Updated:** July 26, 2026

---

# Overview

The roadmap defines the planned evolution of the AI Business Assistant Platform.

Rather than pursuing every possible feature at once, development is organized into incremental milestones that continuously improve the platform while maintaining stability, quality, and customer value.

Each phase builds upon the previous one.

---

# Guiding Principles

The roadmap follows these principles:

- Customer value first
- Incremental delivery
- Engineering quality over feature quantity
- Continuous refactoring
- Security by design
- Cloud-first architecture
- Long-term maintainability
